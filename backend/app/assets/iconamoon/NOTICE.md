# IconaMoon Regular

24 selected icons by **Dariush Habibpour**, from the official
[IconaMoon repository](https://github.com/dariushhpg1/IconaMoon), revision
`b568f9845ca633fa30918b31d166a4de9fe7edb2`.

Original SVGs and 192×192 transparent PNGs are bundled unchanged. The runtime
recolors the PNG alpha mask to the selected presentation theme; it does not
redraw the artwork. The mapping to upstream paths is in `app/icon_catalog.py`.
The reproducible importer is `scripts/fetch_iconamoon.py`.

Upstream README grants free use and modification under “Always free!”.
